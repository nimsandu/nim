Question 1 · 20 marks

Academic English conventions

For each of the original ten questions, choose the best register category. The examiner awards one mark for underlining the expression and one for naming the issue. Item (a) is the professor’s completed example.

How to think: scan for slang → phrasal verbs → contractions → I/we/our → rhetorical questions → absolute claims → vague quantities → biased expressions. If the sentence is acceptable, select Formal.
