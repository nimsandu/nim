How to use this guide

Attempt the original question first. Identify which law controls it; sketch the directions; only then reveal the steps. In the links below each solution, slide numbers are the actual PDF slide/page numbers, and the reference-sheet labels match the two-sided A4 handout.

Universal solving order: Known data → coordinate/sign convention → what changes (time / distance / force / energy / momentum) → governing law → components and constraints → evaluate → check direction, units and physical plausibility.
Important finding — Q03: The professor's page prints 24 N·s, but its supplied graph (120 N; 0.05 s rise; plateau to 0.15 s; fall to 0.25 s) integrates to 21 N·s. This guide shows the graph-based calculation and does not quietly reproduce the inconsistent key.
